## Website Structure

### **Homepage** `/`
* The main landing page featuring featured deals, trending stores, and search functionality.

### **Store Pages** `/discount/[domain]`
* Dedicated pages for specific retailers (e.g., `/discount/adidas.co.uk`).
* Contains the collection of active voucher codes and deals for that specific brand.

### **Detail Pages** `/detail/[pid]`
* Dedicated pages for a specific coupon code

### **All Stores by Categories** `/stores`
* all categories list

### **Category Pages** `/tag-[category]`
* Groups stores by industry or type (e.g., `/tag-fashion`).


### **Static & Legal Pages**
* `/about` – Information about the platform.
* `/contact` – Support and inquiry form.
* `/privacy` – Data handling and cookie information.
* `/terms` – User agreement and site usage rules.
* `/imprint` – Copyright protection information.
* `/blog` – Copyright protection information.
